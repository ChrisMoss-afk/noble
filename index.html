Owner-side advisory for what comes next

Build a business that gives you options.

Noble Advisory Group is a Western Canadian advisory firm for owners of established, owner-managed businesses, typically in the $2–7M revenue range. We help you understand what the business is worth, what could make it more valuable, and what needs to happen before you step back, bring in new leadership, or hand it on.

01 · Who we are

A small, selective team on your side of the table.

We are not brokers, accountants, or a volume consulting practice. Our role is to bring experienced perspectives around one owner, one business, and one desired outcome.

02 · Why now

The strongest transitions are prepared before they are urgent.

Health, burnout, leadership changes, family circumstances, market shifts, or unexpected opportunities can compress the timeline. Preparation protects your ability to choose.

03 · What we do

A structured look at the business before pressure builds.

We examine four connected dimensions: strategic direction, current and potential value, how well the business operates without you, and the gaps a buyer or successor would find first.

Clarity first. Action in the right order.

You leave with an integrated view of the business and a practical roadmap for what deserves attention. For owners who continue with Noble, the work stays focused on real outcomes: stronger operations, improved value, greater owner independence, and better positioning when the time comes.

The goal is not simply to prepare for a transition. The goal is to build a business that gives you options.
